### 📊 Insight – Accreditation Dashboard for Higher Education
**Year:** 2025 · **Role:** Database Designer & Data Visualization Developer  
🔗 [View Project](https://github.com/silhi)

**The Problem**  
Universities in Indonesia face significant challenges in preparing for accreditation. Data from lecturers and institutional records is often scattered, inconsistent, and not mapped to the official BAN-PT (National Accreditation Board) standards — making it difficult to identify gaps and generate meaningful reports.

**My Role & What I Did**  
As the person responsible for data architecture and visualization, I:
- **Analyzed accreditation requirements** based on official BAN-PT and other national accreditation standards, identifying which data points were needed and how they should be structured.
- **Collected and processed institutional data** from lecturers and university sources, then transformed it into clean, structured datasets.
- **Designed the database schema** from scratch, ensuring data relationships matched the logic of accreditation indicators.
- **Built API integrations** to connect the back-end data with front-end components.
- **Created interactive data visualizations** using ApexCharts.js and Power BI, translating raw accreditation data into clear dashboards that support decision-making.

**What I Learned**  
Working with real accreditation standards taught me how to bridge the gap between bureaucratic requirements and technical implementation. I learned how to ask the right questions when gathering data from non-technical stakeholders and how to design a system that's both accurate and usable.

**Tech Stack:** PHP, Laravel, MySQL, PostgreSQL, ApexCharts.js, Power BI, Excel

### 📱 ISRead – Library Mobile Application
**Year:** 2024 · **Role:** UI Designer & Admin-Side Developer  
🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/Silhi/ISRead)

**The Problem**  
Students at the faculty library had no digital way to browse, borrow, or manage books. Everything was manual — which was inefficient for both students and library administrators.

**My Role & What I Did**  
I was responsible for the entire admin-side experience of the application:
- **Designed all UI screens** for the admin module from scratch — from login screens to dashboards — using Figma before implementation.
- **Built admin authentication** with secure login flows and session management.
- **Developed full CRUD functionality** for book management: adding, editing, deleting, and organizing the book collection.
- **Implemented lending management** so admins could track borrowing history, due dates, and book availability in real-time.
- **Created search and filtering features** to help admins quickly locate books and student records.

**Ideas & Design Exploration**  
Before coding, I mapped out the admin's daily workflow to understand what they actually needed on each screen. I prioritized reducing the number of taps required for common tasks like adding a book or confirming a return — so the experience felt efficient, not clunky.

**Tech Stack:** Flutter, Dart, Firebase, Figma

### 🤝 Aksi Kita – Volunteer Campaign Platform
**Year:** 2024 · **Role:** Authentication Developer & UI/Information Designer  
🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/Silhi/Aksi-Kita-Project)

**The Problem**  
Volunteer and social action campaigns in Indonesia often lack a centralized digital space where people can discover events, register, and receive verification of their participation.

**My Role & What I Did**  
I focused on two areas: making the platform trustworthy through secure access, and making it informative through clear design.
- **Built the authentication system**: user registration, login, and session handling — ensuring that only verified users could access campaign features.
- **Designed the volunteer information architecture**: how campaign details, timelines, and impact descriptions were laid out so users could quickly decide whether to join.
- **Developed digital certificate upload/download** functionality so volunteers could save proof of their participation.
- **Designed key UI screens** with a focus on clarity and accessibility for a general public audience.

**Design Thinking Approach**  
I thought about the user's mindset: someone scrolling through campaigns probably has limited time and wants to quickly know *"what is this, when is it, and how do I join."* That shaped how I structured the information hierarchy on every campaign page.

**Tech Stack:** PHP, Laravel, MySQL, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap
